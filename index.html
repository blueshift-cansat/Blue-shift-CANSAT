MISSION 01 PRIMARY
⌁

Required mission

BlueShift will launch a CanSat weighing under 1 kg to approximately 1,000 m. It will demonstrate gyroscopic descent control and a ground-controlled parachute opening at 600 m, while measuring air temperature and pressure and transmitting data to the ground station every second. The CanSat must remain intact throughout the mission.

MISSION 02 TEAM-CHOSEN
⌖

Our secondary mission

Our secondary mission investigates how atmospheric radiation varies with altitude and its potential impact on commercial electronics. High-energy particles can interact with semiconductor devices, potentially causing Single Event Upsets (SEUs), where stored memory bits unexpectedly change state. By exploring the relationship between radiation exposure and memory errors, we aim to better understand electronic reliability in aerospace environments
